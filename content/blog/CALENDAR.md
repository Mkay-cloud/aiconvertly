# Blog Content Calendar

Editorial planning doc, not a published article — excluded from
`content/blog`'s post-scanning in `src/lib/blog.ts` (see `CALENDAR_FILE`
there), so it never shows up on the blog index or sitemap.

27 topics from keyword research, covering the site's whole file-conversion/
media/productivity niche broadly — not limited to topics with an exact
matching tool. **Related Tool Slug** is left blank where no tool fits;
those articles are still worth writing as genuinely useful, honest,
purely informational content.

The first 12 rows (in order) are the **active schedule** for month one.
The remaining 15 are **banked backlog**, to be scheduled after the first
month's results are reviewed. Every row starts at status Pending
regardless of which group it's in.
